CASE: Justice K.S. Puttaswamy (Retd.) v. Union of India (2017)

FACTS:
A group of petitioners challenged the constitutional validity of the Aadhaar scheme, claiming that it violated the fundamental right to privacy.

JUDGMENT:
The Supreme Court of India unanimously held that the right to privacy is a fundamental right under Article 21 of the Constitution.

KEY POINTS:
- Privacy is intrinsic to life and personal liberty.
- The decision overruled previous judgments that denied privacy as a fundamental right.
- The ruling influenced future data protection and surveillance cases in India.

CITATION: (2017) 10 SCC 1
